# Privacy Policy

SDK LinkedIn connects ChatGPT users to LinkedIn so they can publish content they explicitly request.

## Data processed

The service may process the LinkedIn identity returned by OAuth, OAuth access tokens, token expiry information, post text, image URLs supplied for publishing, and identifiers returned for posts created through the service.

## Purpose

Data is used only to authenticate the connected LinkedIn member, execute requested publishing actions, maintain the connection, prevent OAuth abuse, and support user-requested history or edit-learning features when those features are enabled.

## Storage and security

OAuth credentials and tokens must be stored encrypted at rest in production. Secrets are never committed to the public source repository. Access is isolated per connected user.

## Sharing

Data is sent to LinkedIn only as necessary to authenticate or perform the requested LinkedIn action. Infrastructure providers may process encrypted or operational data as necessary to host the service.

## Retention and deletion

Users must be able to disconnect their LinkedIn account and request deletion of stored LinkedIn credentials and associated service data. Production deployment must expose and document that deletion path before public submission.
